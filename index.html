About us

A Ruiru factory that builds roofs to last.

Shadow Mabati Factory is a Kenyan roofing manufacturer and supplier on the Ruiru Bypass, at Kamakis near Karunguru Plaza. We roll-form pre-painted and Aluzinc steel into box profile, tile, corrugated and stone-coated roofing, and we make the ridges, gutters and flashings that finish the job.

We started with one idea: every Kenyan family deserves a roof that is measured honestly, priced openly and built to outlast the rains. That's why we cut every sheet to length, print the gauge on every order, and talk you through the right choice even when it is the cheaper one.
